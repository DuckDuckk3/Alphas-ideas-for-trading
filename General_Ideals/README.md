# Idea 1 — Information Confirmation
## Hypothesis
Multiple independent information sources confirming the same direction indicate stronger signals than any individual source.
## Datasets:
Analyst
News
Option
## Example:
EPS revision + News sentiment  
Target price revision + IV skew  
Recommendation change + Put-call ratio  
EPS revision + News intensity

# Idea 3 — Smart Money Consensus
## Hypothesis
Different informed investors reaching the same conclusion increases the probability that information has not yet been fully reflected in prices.
## Datasets:
Insider
Institutions
Option
## Example: 
Insider buying   
Institutional accumulation   
Bullish IV skew

# Idea 10 — Orthogonal Families

Nếu mục tiêu là có 50–100 Power Pool Alpha, hãy thiết kế các family ít chồng lấn:

Family	Dataset
A	Analyst + Fundamental
B	Analyst + Earnings
C	Option + News
D	Insider + Institutions
E	Short Interest + News
F	Macro + Fundamental
G	Sentiment + Analyst
H	Social + News
I	Option + Short Interest
J	Other + Fundamental

Mỗi family chỉ sử dụng 2–3 datasets và phát triển khoảng 10–15 alpha. Cách này giúp giảm self-correlation tốt hơn nhiều so với việc tạo 100 alpha từ cùng một cặp dữ liệu.
