# Why it works

Fundamental data measures a firm's intrinsic value, analyst estimates provide forward-looking expectations, and news captures the speed of information dissemination. When improving fundamentals are confirmed by analyst revisions and positive news sentiment, the market may still underreact, creating persistent alpha opportunities.

# Research directions

Earnings Yield + EPS Revision + News Sentiment
CFO Yield + Analyst Revision + News Volume
Revenue Growth + Estimate Revision + News Surprise
