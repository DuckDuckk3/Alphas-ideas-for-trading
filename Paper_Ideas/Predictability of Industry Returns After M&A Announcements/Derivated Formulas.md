## The research identifies a significant "industry return drift" following M&A announcements.
The core investment logic is:
- Signal: Calculate the average Cumulative Abnormal Return (CAR) of all M&A announcements within a specific industry during the previous month.
- Strategy: Investors should buy (long) industries that exhibited positive average announcement reactions and sell (short) industries that showed negative average reactions.
- Finding: Capital markets tend to underreact to industry-wide information provided by these announcements, allowing for a profitable monthly long-short strategy.
